# NNNN. Title in the words of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed, Accepted, or Superseded by NNNN (a link to the record that supersedes it)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Requirements:** the requirement IDs this decision serves or revises

## Context

What forces the decision: the requirements, the constraints of the platform, what was learned.

## Decision

What is decided, stated so that code and tests can be checked against it.

## Alternatives

Each alternative weighed, and why it was not taken.

## Consequences

What follows: what becomes easy, what becomes hard, what is now guaranteed and by which tests.

## Migration impact

What changing or reversing this decision would require of hosts, saved layouts, modules and the
framework's own packages.
